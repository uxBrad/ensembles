# Templates

Two templates are provided:

- **`ensemble-template.md`** — The full ensemble persona document, ready to fill in.
- **`lightweight-template.md`** — Just the 2-3 sentence prose version, useful for early drafting or for sharing without the full doc.

## How to use

1. Copy the full template to wherever you're working (your knowledge base, your repo, your local files).
2. Rename it for your specific ensemble (e.g., `acme-product-team.md`).
3. Fill it in following the methodology in [../docs/methodology.md](../docs/methodology.md).
4. Cite evidence per field. Leave gaps explicit rather than fabricating content.

## Recommended fill order

The template is organized for reading. The natural order for *filling* it is different:

1. Lightweight version (the prose paragraph)
2. Roster (Section 2)
3. Repertoire (Section 4)
4. Topology (Section 3)
5. Stimulus-Response (Section 5)
6. Identity (Section 1) — last, because it summarizes the rest

See methodology.md for detail on why this order works.

## Cite evidence

Every field should ideally have a brief evidence note: which interview, observation, or artifact supports it. Light citations are fine — `[INT-03, INT-07]` or `[from team observation 2024-09-15]`. The point is traceability, not formal citation.

If a field has no supporting evidence, leave it empty or mark it `[insufficient research]`. Do not fill empty fields with plausible-sounding content. The framework's value depends on the documents being grounded.

## When in doubt

- **Field feels redundant?** Skip it. Not every field is essential for every ensemble.
- **Field doesn't quite fit?** Adapt it. The schema is a guide, not a constraint.
- **Have research that doesn't fit any field?** Add a custom sub-section. Document why you needed it; if the same need recurs across multiple ensembles, propose it as a schema addition.
